CaseWeave ActionScope is a proposed coordination layer that helps authorised officers review potential relationships between institution-related cases, understand the signals, and coordinate follow-up without replacing official case systems.

The approach

From scattered records
to accountable follow-up.

One focused workflow for surfacing possible connections while preserving officer judgement, source context, and the authority of existing systems.

01 / CONNECT

Authorised case inputs

Bring permitted case attributes into a consistent review view through approved adapters or prototype data.

02 / COMPARE

Potential relationship signals

Compare institution, unit, action scope, type, status and dates to identify records worth examining.

03 / EXPLAIN

Visible matching reasons

Show which attributes triggered a possible relationship instead of presenting a black-box conclusion.

04 / VERIFY

Source freshness checks

Surface stale or unknown source status so missing data is not mistaken for a clean result.

05 / REVIEW

Officer remains in control

Authorised officers accept or reject a signal and record a reason for the decision.

06 / COORDINATE

Track follow-up

Create a coordination task and retain an audit trail while official case records remain authoritative.

Proposed workflow

A signal is a prompt,
not a verdict.

Every candidate relationship flows through human review. Uncertain or stale data is explicitly identified.
